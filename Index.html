Jira Data Retrieval Method & Queried Information
Click to expand

Actual effort is queried dynamically via the Jira Cloud REST API. The backend scans Jira Agile boards for active and closed sprints matching the selected quarter, fetches all child issues in status category Done assigned to those sprints, sums their completed Story Points (customfield_10037), attributes effort to the responsible child Team (primary: customfield_10001, falling back to iTeam customfield_10128), and links them to their parent Jira Epics. Parent epic metadata captures Epic Quarter (customfield_10217), release Fix Versions, labels, and the strategic Epic Bucket (customfield_12204).
